# ICP: catholic-br

Brazilian Catholics, 25 to 55, who take their faith seriously but never had formal formation.
Many attend Mass weekly, pray the rosary, and feel unprepared when a Protestant relative,
coworker or social-media commenter challenges them. They want clear, sourced answers they can
repeat: Scripture with the original language, the Church Fathers, the Magisterium.

They save and forward content that gives them:
- an "I never knew that" fact about the Bible, the canon or Church history;
- a ready answer to a common objection;
- a clear doctrinal point to pass on to family.

Register: prophetic-pedagogical. Attack the error, never the person.

# Caption notes: catholic-br

Notes for the caption-fix pass. The instructions are in English; every spelling below is the
Portuguese form the captions use. The owner adds to this file over time; any change here makes the
next `shorts fixwords` run ask the model again.

## Tradition and canon

- The speaker teaches as a Catholic, to Brazilian Catholics. Read every passage in that frame.
- The Bible is the Catholic canon of 73 books: 46 in the Old Testament and 27 in the New Testament.
  The Old Testament includes the deuterocanonical books (deuterocanônicos): Tobias, Judite,
  1 Macabeus, 2 Macabeus, Sabedoria, Eclesiástico (also called Sirácida) and Baruc, plus the Greek
  parts of Ester and Daniel.
- Frequent sources: the Church Fathers (Agostinho, Jerônimo, Orígenes, Irineu, João Crisóstomo,
  Justino), the Magisterium (Magistério, Catecismo, Denzinger, Concílio de Trento, Vaticano II),
  the Greek Old Testament (Septuaginta), the Hebrew text (Texto Massorético, massoretas), the Latin
  Bible (Vulgata), the Dead Sea Scrolls (Manuscritos do Mar Morto, Qumran).

## Scripture quotations

- A quotation is context, not a target. Fix what was misheard; never rewrite the speaker's words to
  match a Bible translation. If the speaker paraphrases a verse, the paraphrase stays.
- References stay as spoken. "capítulo quinze, versículo trinta e quatro" is not turned into
  digits, and digits are not turned into words.

  Juízes, Rute, Samuel, Reis, Crônicas, Esdras, Neemias, Tobias, Judite, Ester, Macabeus, Jó,
  Salmos, Provérbios, Eclesiastes, Cântico dos Cânticos, Sabedoria, Eclesiástico, Isaías,
  Jeremias, Lamentações, Baruc, Ezequiel, Daniel, Oseias, Joel, Amós, Abdias, Jonas, Miqueias,
  Naum, Habacuc, Sofonias, Ageu, Zacarias, Malaquias; Mateus, Marcos, Lucas, João, Atos dos
  Apóstolos, Romanos, Coríntios, Gálatas, Efésios, Filipenses, Colossenses, Tessalonicenses,
  Timóteo, Tito, Filêmon, Hebreus, Tiago, Pedro, Judas, Apocalipse.
- Biblical and church names with their Portuguese accents: Moisés, Abraão, Isaac, Jacó, Davi,
  Salomão, Elias, Eliseu, Jesus, Maria, José, Pedro, Paulo, João Batista, Pôncio Pilatos, Herodes,
  Caifás, Melquisedec, Agostinho, Jerônimo, Orígenes.
- Hebrew, Greek, Aramaic and Latin words are written the way a Brazilian reader pronounces them,
  with accents on the stressed syllable when the Portuguese Bibles use them, and never translated.
- Latin phrases keep Latin spelling without accents: "ex cathedra", "Dei Verbum",
  "Lumen Gentium", "sola scriptura", "sola fide".
- The speaker's register stays: "pra", "tá", "né", "a gente" are not corrected.

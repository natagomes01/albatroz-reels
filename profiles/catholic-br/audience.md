# Audience and anti-audience: catholic-br

## Audience
Catholics in formation (see icp.md). They react with saves, shares and "amém" comments.

## Anti-audience
People who comment to rebut. Their comments raise reach, as long as the claim is doctrinally
solid and not quote-mined.

- Protestants (evangelical, Pentecostal, Reformed) and ex-Catholics.
- Catholics from rival camps: strict traditionalists, progressives, sedevacantists.
- Skeptics and atheists who attack the Bible's reliability.

## Triggers (what makes the anti-audience comment)
- sola scriptura and the authority of Tradition
- Marian dogmas: perpetual virginity, Immaculate Conception, Assumption, Mary as Mother of God
- the papacy and Peter as the rock
- the Real Presence in the Eucharist
- purgatory and prayers for the dead
- the deuterocanonical books and the formation of the canon
- intercession of the saints and images
- historical-critical scholarship on authorship (Moses, Daniel)

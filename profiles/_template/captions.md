# Caption notes: <channel id>

Notes for the caption-fix pass, which corrects words the speech-to-text misheard. Write the
instructions in English; write every spelling in the language the captions use. Any change here
makes the next `shorts fixwords` run ask the model again.

## Frame
- Who speaks, to whom, and in which tradition or field. One or two lines.

## Names and terms
- Proper names, books, tools and jargon you say often, spelled the way captions must show them.

## Common mishearings
- "<what the transcript wrote>" -> "<what you said>"
